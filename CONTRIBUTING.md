# Contribution licensing

Submit contributions under the license already applying to the affected paths,
as specified in [LICENSING.md](LICENSING.md) and `REUSE.toml`:

- Public SDK, inline/static helpers, import definitions, Make templates and
  minimal example: MIT.
- Covered loader/runtime/resolver and other GPL material: GPL-3.0-only together
  with the existing game and independent-plugin additional permissions.

Contributors retain their copyright; no copyright assignment is required.
Submitting a contribution indicates your intention to license it on those
terms and that you have the rights to do so. State any different proposed terms
explicitly before inclusion. Maintainers must not assume that unrelated GPL code
from another author carries the project's additional permissions.

Preserve upstream notices and identify any copied or adapted third-party
material, its source and its license. Do not submit game binaries/assets or
material for which you cannot supply suitable permission. Keep project copyright
notices limited to original contributions and modifications.
